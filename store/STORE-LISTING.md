### Full description  (max 4000)
PULSE Insight is a colorful block puzzle game with a twist: your Pulse is a heartbeat that keeps draining, and clearing lines pumps it back up. Drop blocks, blast rows and columns, chain combos and level up before you flatline!

If you love classic block puzzles, brain games and relaxing-but-tense grid games, PULSE Insight gives you the same satisfying "one more game" feeling with real stakes and smart power-ups.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8x8 board, or tap a block and tap the board
• Fill a full row or column to clear it
• Clear several lines at once for big combos: GREAT, EXCELLENT, AMAZING, UNBELIEVABLE!
• Your Pulse drains every second, so keep clearing to stay alive
• Fill the Pulse meter to the top to LEVEL UP. Each level is faster, with bigger blocks
• Run out of room or let your Pulse hit zero and it's game over

POWER-UPS
• Insight: shows you which placements will trap you, so you never make a dead-end move
• Surge: your next line clear scores double points
• Blast: tap any block to destroy a 3x3 area and escape a tight spot
Powers cost a little Pulse, so timing is everything.

DAILY CHALLENGES
• 3 new challenges every day: easy, medium and hard
• Earn Boosts that start your next game with extra Pulse and a free Surge
• Finish all 3 to build your daily streak
• Track your best score for today, this week and all-time

FEATURES
• Fast, satisfying block puzzle gameplay for kids and adults
• Glossy candy blocks, particle effects, combo streaks and level-up celebrations
• Original music and sound effects, with haptic feedback
• Themes: Classic, Neon Night, plus seasonal events like the Halloween Spooky theme
• Colorblind-friendly block symbols and a Reduce Motion option
• Quick tutorial for new players
• Play offline, no Wi-Fi needed
• Share your high scores with friends

Simple to learn, hard to master. Every second counts. Can you keep the beat alive?

## Keyword notes
- Main terms used in names/descriptions: block puzzle, block blast, brain games, line clear, combo, offline, 8x8 grid, daily challenge, classic, casual.
- Apple ignores words already in the Name and Subtitle, so the Keywords field avoids repeating "PULSE", "Insight", "puzzle" and "beat".
- Trademarked game names (for example other block games) are left out on purpose. Both stores reject listings that use them.
- These terms are common in the puzzle category. For search-volume numbers, check Google Play Console > Store performance and App Store Connect > App Analytics after a couple of weeks and swap in the terms that bring installs.
