PULSE Insight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

PULSE Insight (“the App”) is a single-player puzzle game published by Digital AIM / btbrepresenta.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or contacts for gameplay.

Your high score may be stored locally on your device only (for example via on-device storage). That data stays on your phone unless you uninstall the App or clear app data.

Advertising

The App may show ads through Google AdMob (including rewarded video and interstitial ads). AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

See Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

Internet

An internet connection is used to load ads. Core puzzle gameplay can still run without ads if none are available.

Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
