Rumble Rift Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rumble Rift is a game made for players of all ages. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, brawlers, trophies, the name you type for online rooms, and your custom maps) is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online rooms

When you choose to play with friends, the game connects your device directly to your friends' devices. To set up that connection, a free relay service (PeerJS) briefly handles a random room ID and the network addresses needed to connect. The room ID is random and not linked to you. The game has no text or voice chat, only preset emotes.

No ads, no tracking, no purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Children

Because we do not collect personal information, the game is suitable for children. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.
