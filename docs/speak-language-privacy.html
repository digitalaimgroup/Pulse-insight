Speak Language Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026 · Digital AIM

Speak Language Game is a practice app. It is built to keep your voice and progress on your device.

What we collect

We do not create accounts, run ads, or sell data. The app does not require your name, email, or phone number.

Microphone

If you allow the microphone, recordings are used only so you can hear yourself say the English word. Recordings stay on your device and are not uploaded. You can skip recording and still play.

On-device storage

Language, topic, and which words you have practiced are saved on your device so the next round remembers where you left off. Clearing the app removes this.

Children

The game is made for language practice, including learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
