Hear a word in English. Record yourself. Then hear it in another language.

1. English Tap the top band to hear the word. That is the prompt you say out loud.
2. Yourself Tap the middle band and speak. Play it back to check. The microphone is optional.
3. Language Tap the bottom band to hear the same word in your chosen language.

Topics & languages

Use the topic chip to switch Animals, Food, Home, Nature, Body, or Travel. Use the language chip for Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Arabic, or Hindi.

Troubleshooting

If sound is quiet, unmute in the header and turn off silent mode on the device. If recording is blocked, allow the microphone in Settings.
