Word Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Digital AIM

Word Rush is a casual offline word-search game. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

No accounts

Word Rush does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or profile data through the game itself.

Data stored on your device

High score, best level, and similar progress are saved locally on your device (for example via local storage or equivalent). This data stays on your device unless you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

Internet and advertising

The core game is designed to work offline. We may add optional advertising in a future update. If ads are introduced, an ad network may collect device identifiers or similar data under that partner’s own policy. We will update this page if advertising is enabled.

Children

Word Rush is a general-audience word puzzle. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
