Word Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Digital AIM

Word Rush Puzzle (“the App”) is a casual word-search game for iOS and Android, published by Digital AIM. This policy explains what information the App does and does not handle.

No accounts

Word Rush does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, or profile data through the game itself.

Data stored on your device

High score, best level, and similar progress are saved locally on your device (for example via local storage or equivalent). This data stays on your device unless you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (including rewarded video and interstitial ads). AdMob and its partners may collect or use device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID or the iOS Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA when available), approximate location derived from IP, and interaction data to show and measure ads.

You can manage advertising identifiers and related settings on your platform:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Google settings / Ads (or Privacy & security → Ads) to reset or limit your advertising ID.

See Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

Internet

An internet connection is used to load ads. Core word-search gameplay can still run if ads are unavailable.

Children

Word Rush is a general-audience word puzzle and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
